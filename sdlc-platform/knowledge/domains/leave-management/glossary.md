# Leave Management — domain glossary

Agents use this glossary for shared vocabulary. It is **not** a source of requirements. Every requirement must trace back to the CRD (`C-nnn`), to reviewer feedback (`REVIEW-n`), or to a checklist item flagged `assumed`.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Leave type | A category of absence with its own rules, e.g. annual/earned, sick, casual, unpaid (LOP), maternity, paternity, compensatory off. |
| Entitlement | The number of days of a leave type an employee may take in a leave year. |
| Accrual | A periodic credit of leave, e.g. 1.5 days per month, often pro-rated for new joiners. |
| Leave balance | Entitlement plus accrued days, minus approved/consumed days, minus pending requests (if the policy holds pending days). |
| Carry forward | Unused balance moved into the next leave year, usually capped. |
| Lapse | Unused balance forfeited at year end. |
| Encashment | Unused balance converted to pay. |
| Half day | A request covering the first or second half of a working day. |
| Holiday calendar | A list of non-working public holidays per location, excluded from the leave-day count. |
| Approver / reporting manager | The person who approves or rejects a request; may be multi-level (manager, then HR). |
| Delegation | A temporary reassignment of approval authority while the approver is away. |
| Escalation | Automatic routing of a request to the next approver after a timeout. |
| Loss of pay (LOP) | Unpaid leave that reduces salary; it must reach payroll. |
| Leave year | The accounting period for entitlements: calendar year, financial year or joining anniversary. |

## Typical roles
- **Employee:** applies for, views and cancels their own leave.
- **Manager:** approves or rejects requests and views the team calendar.
- **HR Admin:** configures leave types, policies and holidays, and adjusts balances.
- **System Admin:** manages users and roles.
